# Anti-Slop Audit — Admin document review panel

Scope: the panel displayed after selecting **Periksa** in `src/pages/Admin.tsx` and its related styles in `src/admin.css`. The review queue and its filters are out of scope.

## Findings

1. **LOW — Review layout does not prioritize the review decision (R-05).** The review panel is rendered inline after the queue, with each indicator and its note stacked vertically. This makes the work area compete with surrounding page content instead of structuring the layout around the administrator's review task.

## Follow-up

Finding #1 was approved and resolved. The review panel now opens as a modal with program navigation, per-indicator revision notes, and the original approval/revision handlers. The review queue and filters were left unchanged.

Validation: `npm.cmd run build` and `npm.cmd exec -- oxfmt --check src\\pages\\Admin.tsx src\\admin.css` pass. The existing type-check and workflow test commands fail on syntax errors in untouched files, including `src/utils/workflow.ts`; those unrelated files were not changed.
